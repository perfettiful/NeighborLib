The ways it actually goes

Four branches, and every one of them ends somewhere defined. 3

takes it without scanning anything
The list is wrong until the curator notices a gap on the shelf and taps mark out, or until it turns up back in the box and nobody ever knew. The most common path by far.
brings it back without tapping
Same thing in reverse. The copy reads as out for weeks, then the curator sees it on the shelf and marks it in. The reminder email is the main defense against this one.
never comes back
Sits as out, rolls past a few weeks into the red, and eventually the curator marks it gone from the dashboard. No fee, no email, no record of who.
keeps it four months, then returns it
The I put it back link never expires, so the copy goes from gone to on the shelf. Every state in this product is reversible for exactly this reason. 4
