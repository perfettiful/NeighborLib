Two roles, no staff

A curator keeps one box and holds the only kind of account in the system. A passerby is everybody else, with no account, no name and no history. There's no staff tier, no moderator, and no organization behind any of it.

Everything runs on the honor system. No late fees, no due dates, no borrowing limits, nothing to suspend. That's a decision with consequences all over the design, and it's argued out on how this works.
